FAQ

Core questions

Can PHN be mined?

No. PHN is fixed supply; verified nodes earn from the reward pool.

Is PHN already cross-chain?

Not yet. peaq is live first; BSC comes later through a one-supply route.

Why require identity?

Because machine economies need accountability to resist sybil farming and reward abuse.

Where is the logo stored?

In project metadata and token-list submissions, not inside token bytecode.
