Skymetric Consulting LLP

A verified machine economy with a bigger Web3 roadmap.

Peaq Human Node (PHN) is built by Skymetric Consulting LLP as a peaq-first DePIN protocol. Machines do not earn anonymously; they earn after identity, operator, and heartbeat checks.

What PHN is

PHN is the node reward, staking, and utility layer for verified human-operated compute nodes.

Economy model

PHN uses presale epochs, transparent staking, vesting, burn fees, treasury routing, and future governance controls.

Liquidity plan

The priority is peaq-first liquidity, then stablecoin routes, then BNB and cross-chain expansion after the base is stable.

Future ecosystem

The roadmap expands toward Skymetric Chain, SKY utility token, SKYGOV governance, and games that use verified identity as a core mechanic.

Long-Term Direction

  • Build Skymetric Chain as a future EVM-compatible chain for verified DePIN and games.
  • Launch games such as NodeRunner, Proof of Human, and DePIN Wars after PHN has traction.
  • Introduce SKY as a future utility token for gas, app fees, games, and ecosystem services.
  • Introduce SKYGOV as an earned governance credential for contributors, stakers, and verified node operators.
