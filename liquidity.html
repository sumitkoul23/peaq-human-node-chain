DEX Liquidity Sequence

  1. Fund presale inventory.
  2. Let presale collect PEAQ into treasury.
  3. Reserve PHN inventory for pool creation.
  4. Create PHN/PEAQ pool when router/pair is selected.
  5. Lock LP tokens and publish lock transaction.
Note: BSC PancakeSwap liquidity is blocked until the BSC PHN contract is deployed and the deployer has BNB gas.

Presale Liquidity

Presale creates immediate buy-side access while treasury accumulates PEAQ for later pool depth.

Staking Liquidity

Funded staking reduces circulating pressure by giving holders a reason to lock tokens.

DEX Liquidity

DEX pool comes after enough PEAQ and PHN are reserved, then LP is locked for trust.
