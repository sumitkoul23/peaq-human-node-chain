Games

Planned games include NodeRunner, Proof of Human, and DePIN Wars. Games are designed to create utility loops for node identity, PHN rewards, and future SKY usage.

Utility Tokens

PHN remains the node reward and staking token. SKY is planned as a future chain utility token for gas, apps, games, and ecosystem fees.

Governance Tokens

SKYGOV is planned as an earned, soulbound governance credential rather than a speculative market token.

Own Chain

Skymetric Chain is planned after PHN has proven demand, liquidity, node traction, and community operations.
